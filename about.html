About this website.

I made this website to show off some of my favorite characters I own. Some I created, some I purchased from designers or other character owners, some were gifts. By owning these characters I am allowed to use them for whatever my heart desires. Whether that be drawing them, commissioning art of them, using them in stories, games, or roleplays, or alternate universes of their lore.

The theme I chose is loosely based off of a color palette I found on the internet themed after a coral reef. I love coral reefs, sharks, and all things ocean-related, so choosing this was a no-brainer. I changed and added a few colors to make it more cohesive in a website. Also because my username is starfell_reef on pretty much every social I have, I figured a reef/ocean theme would work best. On a related note: my favorite ocean animal is the Dudgeon's Walking Shark - discovered January of 2026. It's the newest member of the Epaulette or walking shark genus.

As the digital spaces I tend to stick around in encourage giving credit where credit is due, I also have credits attatched to all of the art that I didn't make. A few of the artists I have linked have shown up in my character galleries more than once. I really like supporting small artists and also getting pretty art of my characters.
