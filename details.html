KELP
  • Species: Dragon (Seawing rainwing hybrid)
  • Age: 21
  • Pronouns: He/they
  • Trivia:
    • Kelp is a fandom character for the book series Wings of Fire
    • He's a hybrid of two types of dragons called seawings and rainwings
    • I have so many characters for this fandom, but Kelp is my main character I use

Design backstory

I don't remember when exactly I made Kelp as a design, but he's been through one redesign in the many years I've had him. His colors aren't colorpicked from anything as far as I know. Though the original reference sheet I made is a base that I edited. Because he's a hybrid of two dragon types, or tribes as they're called in the books, I had to cut parts of two bases and combine them to my liking. His design is also slightly malleable, but there are a few things that are required to follow the reference. I'm still super proud of the design.

Lore

Kelp's lore is pretty simple, I only have about three paragraphs of backstory.

Childhood

He hatched rather cared for. His parents loved him and took good care of him. He's the youngest of three, but they all feel equally loved and cared for. The only thing really traumatic about his childhood was that his mother died of an illness before he was five. He doesn't remember a lot about her.

Adolescence

Going to school was a basic day-to-day task for Kelp, at least until he met a cute guy named Haifisch. They quickly became good friends, spending time outside of school together, including on weekends.

Adulthood

Kelp and Haifisch moved in together when they both got out of high school. Living together was at first stressful, as Kelp didn't know what made Haifisch upset. Eventually, though, he learned how to keep him happy, and Haifisch did the same to help Kelp.
